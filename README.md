## 🚨 Overview

**NERVES (Northeast Emergency Response, Visibility & Evacuation Support)** is an AI-powered disaster response and logistics platform designed to improve emergency coordination across the geographically challenging and disaster-prone regions of Northeast India.

NERVES combines:

- 🗺️ GIS-based situational awareness
- 🧠 Explainable AI
- 🛣️ Safety-first intelligent routing
- 🚑 Fleet and convoy management
- 📦 Essential supply management
- 📍 Geo-tagged incident reporting
- 📡 Offline-first field operations
- 🚨 Targeted emergency alerts
- 👥 Role-based access control
- 🌐 Multilingual support
- 🔮 What-If disaster simulation
- 📊 Command-center analytics
- 📝 Audit trails
- 🚗 Driver cockpit
- 👨‍🚒 NDRF/SDRF field operations

The goal is simple:

> **NERVES doesn't find the shortest route; it finds the safest operationally accessible route.**

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Disaster response in Northeast India faces several challenges:

- Frequent floods, landslides and road blockages
- Mountainous and difficult terrain
- Limited road accessibility
- Network/connectivity failures
- Fragmented information between agencies
- Difficulty coordinating fleets and supplies
- Delayed field reporting
- Lack of unified situational awareness
- Routing systems that prioritize distance/time instead of disaster-specific operational safety

During an emergency, the shortest route is not necessarily the route that will successfully deliver people, medicine or supplies.

A route may be:

- shorter but flooded,
- faster but restricted,
- accessible only to certain vehicles,
- affected by landslides,
- unsafe for heavy convoys,
- disconnected from emergency support.

NERVES addresses these challenges through a unified disaster-response intelligence platform.
